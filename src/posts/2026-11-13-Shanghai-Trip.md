I went to Shanghai and Beijing near the end of October. As someone who loves the cold, the autumn weather was great and perfect for being outdoors. Unfortunately, I spent most of my time inside of random malls.

Whenever I go to China, I have this instinctive urge to search for anime-related places. And Shanghai is kind of the ideal destination for this, since it is probably one of the cities with the highest concentration of malls dedicated to anime stuff (or 二次元 as they call it).

{% imgBlock "/posts/images/2026-oct/5.png", "Yes that is a large Lycahugo billboard for Lycaon's birthday outside this mall" %}

Bailian ZX Creative Centre (百联ZX创趣场) — pictured above — is a fairly famous one that I decided to visit. This isn't actually some random mall though, since it's right on the busiest shopping street in Shanghai.

Inside is basically five full floors of anime merch shops, including shops for official Japanese anime studios. There was an official IG and WIT Studio shop with your typical Attack on Titan and Haikyuu!! merch, but I didn't expect to see the new [LOGH](https://myanimelist.net/anime/31433/Ginga_Eiyuu_Densetsu__Die_Neue_These_-_Kaikou) anime getting featured as well (although there wasn't any actual merch).

{% imgBlock "/posts/images/2026-oct/7.png", "Can they make Season 5 (?) lmfao...." %}

However, my true destination was this — a [Case File Compendium](https://sevenseasentertainment.com/series/case-file-compendium-bing-an-ben-novel/) themed cafe:

{% imgBlock "/posts/images/2026-oct/8.png", "" %}

This is a Chinese BL/ Danmei novel I read earlier this year which lowkey got me back into the genre as a whole. ~~(It also lowkey influenced my decision to go to Shanghai but let's not talk about that _shhhh_)~~

Anyway, I saw this cafe online about a month ago and honestly did not expect it to still be there by the time I was in Shanghai, but somehow it really did run until then. So I simply had to go there...

{% imgBlock "/posts/images/2026-oct/9.png", "" %}

I went on a Monday morning so it was completely empty except for me LMFAO but at least I didn't have to make a booking which would probably have been impossible as a foreigner (don't quote me on that).

{% imgBlock "/posts/images/2026-oct/10.png", "" %}

The food was surprisingly decent, but other than that, I think it's pretty similar to your typical anime cafe, where you order themed food and get a bunch of freebies. There was also a lot of merch for sale, but I didn't buy any because I already got enough of the freebies 😂

On the topic of Hoyoverse games, I'm admittedly not as big of a fan now as I was in the past, but since I was in Shanghai, I had to drop by their headquarters.

In actuality, they have a lot of different office buildings scattered throughout one area. I wanted to check out this Astral Express, and it was an absolute pain in the ass to find.

{% imgBlock "/posts/images/2026-oct/11.png", "" %}

Oh and they only have one office that allows visitors, but only on weekends, and you'll have to make a booking on some WeChat application. Unfortunately, I didn't realise that and I went on a week day, which meant I didn't get to go inside 😅

Of course, this process might change again in the future so don't be like me. Do your research before going to an obscure Chinese business park to visit your favourite Gacha game company 👍 _(Please don't actually go to anyone's office unless it's explicitly allowed.)_

As a consolation, there is a park/ mall area near the Hoyoverse office with a bunch of installations themed around different (Gacha) games, which was pretty cool.

In the bottom left corner are my attempts at making felt dolls of my OCs at these DIY felt doll shops. They work like an IRL Picrew where you have to select the base, different hair parts, clothes and accessories, and then iron them together to make the doll. I don't think I did a good job of making them resemble my characters but I tried my best with the limited selection ;w;

All in all, I don't think I bought much stuff considering the number of merch shops I went to. I've reached the point where it isn't so much the price of the merch holding me back, as the fact that I have to find space to store it!!! Storage space has become more precious than merch to me... Unfortunately, I still like _looking_ at merch, so I can't help but impulse-buy sometimes.
